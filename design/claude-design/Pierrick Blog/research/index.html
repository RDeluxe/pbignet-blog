Four ways to print the same page.

A first look at the visual language for your personal site — retro, paper-like, and built on three shared ingredients. Each direction below is a live homepage. Open one to scroll it, and drop your own photos into the frames to watch the dithering take over.

→ ROUND 2 · BOLD STUDIES  effects baked onto your photos, font-on-picture.

INGREDIENT 01

Paper & grain

Warm off-white stock with a fine film grain and a printed-sheet vignette. The constant across all four.

INGREDIENT 02

Topography

Faint contour lines drawn from value-noise sit behind everything as ambient terrain.

INGREDIENT 03

Animated dither

An 8×8 Bayer dither that gently boils. Ambient before a photo lands, then it dithers the photo itself.

INGREDIENT 04

Type & metadata

Heavy display type, monospace labels, coordinates, registration marks and barcodes for instrument feel.
